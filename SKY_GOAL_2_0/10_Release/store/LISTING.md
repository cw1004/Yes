## English

**App name**
```
SKY GOAL 2.0 : One-Tap Football
```

**Short description**
```
A one-tap football arcade. The AI reads your play and tunes the difficulty to you.
```

**Full description**
```
⚽ One tap. That's the whole game.

Loft the ball through moving goals. One button — but the goals get narrower,
faster, and they move.

🤖 Difficulty that adapts to you
SKY GOAL reads how you play: survival time, pass rate, combo, tap rhythm,
and your recent success rate. Seven signals become a skill score that sets
the next match's difficulty.

Too easy? The goal narrows. Too hard? It opens up.
It never moves more than ±5 per run, so you never hit a wall out of nowhere —
and the result screen tells you exactly why it changed.

🏔 A stadium that changes
Day → Sunset → Night → Rain → Wind → Storm → World Final.
Himalayan ridgelines and a river shift colour with every stage. Lightning
cracks in the storm, wind pushes your ball, and the music stacks up
another layer each time you climb.

🎯 Grow your player
Perfect passes, coins, XP, level-ups, and five stats to spend points on —
CONTROL, POWER, SPEED, LUCK, STAMINA.

⚡ No sign-up. Fully offline. Ads never interrupt a run.
```
